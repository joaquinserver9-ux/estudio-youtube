Estudio YouTube

Estudio YouTube is a private, single-user desktop tool. Its owner uses it to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel and to read that channel's statistics.

What it does

  • Uploads videos to the owner's channel, only after the owner approves each upload.
  • Reads public video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel.
  • Publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram account and reads its statistics, after the owner approves each post.

It is not offered to the public and does not have other users.
